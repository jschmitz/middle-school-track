Practice 3 — Wednesday, August 26

Village Green Loops

Continuous loop running around Village Green with a water break at the baseball field on each lap. Athletes ran for approximately 20 minutes, building their aerobic base and getting comfortable with sustained effort.

1 — Easy Warm-Up Jog 5 min

Standard group warm-up jog to get moving before the main run.

  • Easy jog around the baseball field together at conversational pace
  • Coach leads, full group stays together

2 — Dynamic Warm-Up 8 min

Standard drill sequence. Athletes are building familiarity with the routine.

  • High Knees — 20m
  • Butt Kicks — 20m
  • Frankensteins — 20m
  • A-Skips — 20m
  • Carioca — 20m each direction
  • Leg Swings (front/back and side to side) — 10 reps each leg
  • 2× strides — 60m, build to 80%, walk back

3 — Village Green Loops 20 min

Sustained loop running around Village Green. Water break built in at the baseball field on every lap — keeps athletes hydrated and gives a natural recovery point without fully stopping the workout.

  • Course: Loops around Village Green, passing through the baseball field area each lap
  • Water break: Athletes stop briefly at the baseball field on each lap for water, then continue
  • Duration: Approximately 20 minutes of continuous effort including water breaks
  • Athletes ran at a comfortable, conversational pace — Z2 effort. This is aerobic base building, not racing.
  • Tier 1 athletes could walk portions and still complete laps at their own pace
  • No pressure on speed — the goal is time on feet and building the habit of sustained running

4 — Cool Down & Stretch 7 min

Same sequence every practice.

  • Easy walk to bring heart rate down
  • Standing quad stretch — 20 sec each leg
  • Hamstring stretch — 20 sec each leg
  • Hip flexor lunge — 20 sec each side
  • Calf stretch — 20 sec each leg
  • Team huddle — one thing done well, one thing to work on
