Practice 4 — Friday, August 29

Loops & Cone Drills

Village Green loops with structured cone drill breaks at the baseball field. Athletes worked on running straight lines through cones, reinforcing good form and straight-ahead mechanics while keeping up their aerobic mileage.

1 — Easy Warm-Up Jog 5 min

Standard group warm-up jog before any real effort.

  • Easy jog around the baseball field together at conversational pace
  • Coach leads, full group stays together

2 — Dynamic Warm-Up 8 min

Same drill sequence as previous practices. Athletes are getting into the routine.

  • High Knees — 20m
  • Butt Kicks — 20m
  • Frankensteins — 20m
  • A-Skips — 20m
  • Carioca — 20m each direction
  • Leg Swings (front/back and side to side) — 10 reps each leg
  • 2× strides — 60m, build to 80%, walk back

3 — Village Green Loops with Cone Drill Breaks 20 min

Athletes ran loops around Village Green, stopping at the baseball field between laps for a cone drill before heading back out. The drill reinforced straight-line running mechanics — a useful cue for athletes who tend to drift or weave during races.

  • Course: Loops around Village Green, with a stop at the baseball field on each lap
  • Cone drill: Cones set in a straight line across the baseball field — athletes ran through them in a straight line, focusing on staying on track and driving forward without drifting
  • Format: Run a loop → cone drill at the baseball field → water break → repeat
  • Aerobic effort throughout — conversational pace on the loops, controlled acceleration through the cones
  • Tier 1 athletes could walk portions of the loops and still complete the drill at each break
  • The cone drill is a simple but effective cue for running economy — straight-line efficiency matters on a race course

4 — Cool Down & Stretch 7 min

Same sequence every practice.

  • Easy walk to bring heart rate down
  • Standing quad stretch — 20 sec each leg
  • Hamstring stretch — 20 sec each leg
  • Hip flexor lunge — 20 sec each side
  • Calf stretch — 20 sec each leg
  • Team huddle — one thing done well, one thing to work on
